Explore AI is a demo platform for conversational AI, Document QA (RAG), tools, and quality evaluation.
Core modules include Chat, RAG, Eval, Metrics, Agent, and Vision.
Chat supports multi-provider text models with optional tools such as weather and web search.
Document QA retrieves relevant chunks from uploaded documents and answers grounded in that context.
Eval uses Spring AI RelevancyEvaluator and FactCheckingEvaluator to score answer quality.
